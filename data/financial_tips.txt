========================
BUDGETING CORE RULES
========================

- Follow the 50/30/20 rule:
  50% needs (rent, food, bills),
  30% wants (entertainment, shopping),
  20% savings/investments.

- Always prioritize saving before spending (Pay Yourself First principle).

- Emergency fund should cover 3–6 months of essential expenses.

- Never invest money if emergency fund is not established.

- Fixed expenses (rent, EMI, subscriptions) should not exceed 50–60% of income.

- Track all expenses daily or weekly to avoid hidden leakage.


========================
SPENDING BEHAVIOR INSIGHTS
========================

- Food delivery and online shopping are the most common sources of impulse spending.

- Weekend spending is typically 20–40% higher than weekday spending.

- Small repeated purchases (like snacks, subscriptions) create major monthly leakage.

- Emotional states like stress, boredom, or celebration increase unnecessary spending.

- Subscription services often go unnoticed and accumulate over time.

- People tend to underestimate their “small daily expenses” significantly.


========================
SAVINGS & FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE
========================

- Savings should be automated whenever possible.

- Increase savings rate whenever income increases (avoid lifestyle inflation).

- Maintain at least 20% savings rate minimum for financial stability.

- Build short-term, medium-term, and long-term savings buckets.

- Short-term savings = 0–1 year goals (gadgets, trips)

- Medium-term savings = 1–5 years (car, education)

- Long-term savings = 5+ years (wealth building, retirement)


========================
INVESTMENT RULES
========================

- Younger users (18–25) can take higher equity exposure due to long time horizon.

- Risk-averse users should prefer debt instruments or balanced funds.

- Diversification reduces investment risk significantly.

- Do not invest in high-risk assets without financial stability.

- Start with simple instruments like index funds before advanced trading.

- Avoid emotional investing based on market hype.


========================
RISK MANAGEMENT RULES
========================

- Always maintain liquidity for emergencies.

- Avoid over-dependence on credit cards for lifestyle expenses.

- High debt-to-income ratio increases financial stress and instability.

- If debt exists, prioritize repayment before aggressive investing.

- Financial safety comes before wealth growth.


========================
BEHAVIORAL FINANCE RULES
========================

- Humans are loss-averse and react more strongly to losses than gains.

- People tend to overspend when rewards or cashback are involved.

- Financial decisions are often emotionally influenced, not logical.

- Framing effect influences how users perceive expenses and savings.

- Commitment systems (like fixed savings transfers) improve discipline.


========================
FINANCIAL ADVICE FORMAT RULES
========================

When generating advice, always structure output as:

1. Insights:
   Explain user spending patterns clearly.

2. Risks:
   Highlight financial risks or unhealthy behavior.

3. Action Plan:
   Provide simple, step-by-step improvements.

Keep language simple, practical, and non-technical.

Avoid jargon unless necessary.

Prioritize clarity over complexity.


========================
PERSONALIZATION RULES
========================

- If user is young (18–25): focus on habit building and long-term investing.

- If user has low savings: prioritize budgeting and expense control.

- If user has high income: focus on optimization and investment strategy.

- If user is risk-averse: suggest safe and stable instruments.

- If user overspends in a category: suggest category-specific reduction strategies.


========================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
========================

The goal of financial advice is not just wealth creation,
but building long-term financial stability, discipline, and awareness.
